03 · Featured project

Sentinel Swarm: smart attendance for a whole campus

Sentinel Swarm is a full-stack attendance and access-management system built for the University of Peradeniya. ESP32 and Raspberry Pi nodes collect attendance on their own and send it to a PHP/MySQL dashboard for admins, lecturers and students. We showed it at Pera Nexus 2026.

  • 7 ways to mark attendance: fingerprint (FM10A), RFID, face recognition in the browser and on the Pi, voice, a QR code that changes every 5 seconds, and manual entry
  • Custom hardware: keypad, 16×4 LCD, SD-card offline queue, an 18650 battery with a TP4056 charger and XL6009 boost converter, and MOSFET power gating
  • Mesh and updates: ESP-NOW offline mesh and firmware updates pushed from the dashboard (OTA)
  • Dashboard: live logs, a mirror of the device's LCD, a virtual keypad, analytics, and email alerts when a student falls below 80% attendance
